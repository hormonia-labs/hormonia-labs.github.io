Privacy Policy

LogWell (the "App") is operated by Hormonia ("we", "us"). LogWell is a personal productivity tool that lets a postgraduate medical trainee log their own clinical, procedural, and academic work and export it as a university/board-format logbook. This policy explains what data the App collects, why, how it is protected, and your choices. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Last updated: 15 September 2026.

1. Applicability

This policy applies to the trainees and doctors who use the App to record their own work. LogWell keeps no patient records and is not a patient management system — see section 3.

3. What we deliberately do NOT collect

LogWell is designed to hold no patient-identifiable information. There is no field for a patient's name, phone number, address, medical record number, or date of birth, and there never will be. Age is recorded only as a broad band (e.g. "41–60") and sex only as a category — never an exact identity. The App does not record audio, does not access your contacts, photos, or precise location, contains no advertising, and uses no advertising identifier.

4. How we use your data

  • Store the entries you log and show your progress toward competency targets.
  • Generate your exported logbook (PDF or spreadsheet) with signature blocks.
  • Grant and track one-time export credits.
  • Provide support when you contact us.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your data to train or fine-tune AI models.

5. Data security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. The optional hospital reference is additionally field-encrypted (AES-256-GCM) so it is never stored in readable form. Exported logbook files are served through short-lived, private download links and are automatically deleted from our servers after 7 days. Access to backend systems is restricted and authenticated. In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users and relevant authorities as required by Indian law.

6. How your data is shared

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. We use the following service providers strictly to operate the App:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — secure hosting (Mumbai region).
  • Neon — managed PostgreSQL database.
  • Google Sign-In / Apple Sign-In — authentication.
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase management for export credits.

These providers process data only on our behalf to deliver the App's features.

7. Data retention & deletion

  • Exported files: automatically deleted from our servers after 7 days.
  • Your entries and profile: retained while your account is active.
  • Account deletion: you can delete your account and all its data instantly from within the App (Settings → Delete account), or by email — see the Delete Account page.

8. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us at hormonia.in@gmail.com.

The App is intended for medical trainees and professionals 18 years and older. It is not directed at children.
